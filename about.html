About JionCargo

A cargo studio
for the whole archipelago.

JionCargo is a cargo studio built for a country of more than 7,000 islands. We handle the whole trip — pickup, hub transfers, sea or air crossing and final delivery — so you deal with one team instead of five.

We believe a shipment should never be a mystery. Every cargo carries a waybill, every handover is logged, and every arrival time is posted where you can see it.

From a single parcel in Metro Manila to a pallet bound for Davao, we price it clearly and move it carefully.

Step 01Book & packWe log your cargo and issue a waybill.
Step 02Pickup & hubCollected and sorted at the nearest hub.
Step 03Cross-countryBy road, RoRo vessel or air to the destination hub.
Step 04DeliveredOut for delivery, signed and closed on the tracker.
